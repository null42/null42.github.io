**8.16** Watkins–Johnson 变换器在连续导通模式下的小信号方程为：

$$L\frac{d\hat{i}(t)}{dt} = -D\hat{v}(t) + (2V_g - V)\hat{d}(t) + (D - D')\hat{v}_g(t)$$

$$C\frac{d\hat{v}(t)}{dt} = D\hat{i}(t) - \frac{\hat{v}(t)}{R}$$

$$\hat{i}_g(t) = (D - D')\hat{i}(t) + 2I\hat{d}(t)$$
